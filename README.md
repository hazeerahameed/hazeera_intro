# BEYOND THE BEGINNING
### Personal Introduction Website • Hazeera Hameed
**Course Assignment for Hat Innovations Digital Marketing Course**

---

## ✦ Overview
**"BEYOND THE BEGINNING"** is an elegant, responsive, and cinematic personal introduction website created by **Hazeera Hameed**. 

The website captures the transition from a solid professional grounding in finance and accounting with **Emirates Group** into the dynamic landscape of modern **digital marketing**, weaving personal determination, mentor guidance from **Mr. Hariprasad**, and forward-looking aspirations into a connected, tasteful narrative.

## ✦ Website Story Flow
1. **Cinematic Hero**: Opening statement, *"Sometimes, a new beginning starts with an opportunity. I'm Hazeera Hameed. Welcome to my next chapter."* with ambient particle glow and interactive CTA button.
2. **My Foundation**: Grounding in finance and accounting with Emirates Group — analytical rigor, international benchmarks, and an enduring platform for future capabilities.
3. **An Unexpected Journey**: Interactive 4-stage timeline documenting the first enrollment 3 years ago, balancing late-night sessions with family responsibilities, a respectful inquiry, and returning with renewed clarity.
4. **Guiding Words (Mentor Advice)**: Spotlight on Mr. Hariprasad's positive encouragement:
   - *Pillar I: Make the Most of This Batch*
   - *Pillar II: Complete Assignments Well*
   - *Pillar III: Learn from the Group*
   - *Pillar IV: Remain Open to Opportunities*
5. **Who I Am Today**: 4-quadrant profile balancing professional experience, active curiosity, willingness to learn, and forward determination.
6. **My Goals**: Clear, grounded objectives covering skill acquisition, assignment execution, peer learning, and building sustainable, flexible income avenues.
7. **Final Reflection & Acknowledgement**:
   - *“An opportunity is only the beginning. What I learn, what I create, and what I do with that opportunity — that's up to me.”*
   - Sincere mentor acknowledgement: *“Thank you, Mr. Hariprasad, for encouraging me to make the most of this opportunity.”*
   - Smooth *"Revisit The Beginning"* navigation.

## ✦ Free Online Hosting Instructions (3 Easy Ways)

You can host this website completely free with zero monthly fees or technical setup:

### Method 1: Netlify Drop (Fastest — 30 Seconds, No Account Needed initially)
1. Go to [https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
2. Drag and drop the entire `Hazeera_Intro` folder into the upload circle on your browser.
3. Your website is immediately live with a free SSL certificate and a shareable URL (e.g., `https://beyond-the-beginning.netlify.app`).

---

### Method 2: GitHub Pages (Free Forever)
1. Create a free account at [github.com](https://github.com) if you do not have one.
2. Create a new repository (e.g., `hazeera-intro`).
3. Upload `index.html`, `css/style.css`, and `js/main.js` to the repository.
4. Go to **Settings** > **Pages** in the repository.
5. Under **Branch**, select `main` and root `/`, then click **Save**.
6. Your website will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/hazeera-intro/`.

---

### Method 3: Vercel (Drag & Drop or CLI)
1. Sign up free at [vercel.com](https://vercel.com).
2. Click **Add New** > **Project**.
3. Import your GitHub repository or drag the folder directly.
4. Click **Deploy**.
